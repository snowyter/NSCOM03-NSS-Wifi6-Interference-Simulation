Open through python3 app.py to run the simulator from this page.

Each lane shows transmissions on the channel. A station sends an RTS, the AP answers with a CTS, then the data and its acknowledgement follow. Red marks time when two transmissions overlap, which means a collision and a wasted exchange. With RTS/CTS the colliding frames are short RTS frames, without it they are whole data frames. Long bars are aggregated frames: when the channel is busy a station packs many packets into one transmission, so a single bar can last several milliseconds. Hover a frame for details.
